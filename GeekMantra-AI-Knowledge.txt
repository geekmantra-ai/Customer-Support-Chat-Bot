COMPANY OVERVIEW
GeekMantra AI helps businesses attract, engage, and convert more customers with smart, AI-powered solutions. The company brings together AI assistants, digital marketing, websites, agent workflows, lead generation, and inventory management tools to support business growth.

The goal is to help a business create more useful customer interactions and connect its marketing and operations with practical technology. Solutions can be selected around a business's needs; the services listed here cover customer conversations, online visibility, lead capture, and inventory operations.

LOCATION:
Based out of Hyderabad, India

SERVICES

AI CHAT ASSISTANTS
AI chat assistants help businesses respond to customer questions through chat. They can support customer engagement by providing conversational assistance and helping visitors find information or take a next step. The exact topics, channels, and actions depend on how an assistant is configured for a business.

AI VOICE ASSISTANTS
AI voice assistants provide an AI-powered way for businesses to engage with people through voice conversations. They can be part of a customer-service or lead-engagement workflow. The supported call flows and integrations depend on the business requirements and implementation.

SMART ADS AND DIGITAL MARKETING
GeekMantra AI provides smart advertising and digital marketing services to help businesses reach potential customers online. Digital marketing can support awareness, audience engagement, and conversion goals. Specific platforms, campaign budgets, targeting plans, and performance results are not defined in this knowledge file.

SOCIAL MEDIA MARKETING
Social media marketing helps businesses build and engage an audience through social channels. It is one of GeekMantra AI's listed services and can complement broader digital marketing and advertising efforts. Specific social platforms and publishing schedules are determined for each business.

SMART WEBSITES AND AI AGENT WORKFLOWS
GeekMantra AI creates smart websites and AI agent workflows. A website can give customers a place to learn about a business and its offerings. AI agent workflows can connect conversational assistance with defined business tasks. The exact website features, workflow steps, and software integrations vary according to project requirements.

LEAD GENERATION
Lead generation focuses on helping businesses attract prospective customers and encourage them to express interest. It can work alongside advertising, social media marketing, websites, chat assistants, and voice assistants. The lead qualification criteria and follow-up process depend on the business.

INVENTORY MANAGEMENT PLATFORM
GeekMantra AI offers an inventory management platform. It is intended to support inventory-related business operations. This knowledge file does not specify individual platform features, supported integrations, pricing, or technical requirements; those details should be confirmed with GeekMantra AI.

HOW THE SERVICES FIT TOGETHER
A business may use one or more of these services. For example, digital marketing and social media marketing can help attract an audience; a website can introduce the business; a chat or voice assistant can support customer engagement; and lead generation can help capture prospective-customer interest. The inventory management platform addresses a separate operational need. This is an illustrative relationship between the listed services, not a required package or guaranteed workflow.
